# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Women in their 20s–30s who follow Maria Savage (@mriafitness, 50k+ followers) or found her
through brand collabs (MyProtein, AYBL, Motel Rocks). They arrive mostly on mobile from an
Instagram bio link, half-convinced already. Their job: decide whether Maria's coaching is
for them and start the Lean & Locked plan.

## Product Purpose

Single-page marketing site for Maria Savage, fitness coach and content creator. One job:
convert followers and curious visitors into Lean & Locked sign-ups. About and proof
sections exist only to earn that click. Front-end only for now — no backend, no SEO pass.

## Brand Personality

Confident, warm, direct. First person, a bit cheeky ("Here's the proof →" is her own
copy). Big type that isn't afraid of itself; earthy and editorial rather than neon-gym.
Three words: bold, sun-warmed, personal.

## Anti-references

- Generic "AI website" template feel: identical card grids, eyebrow labels over every
  heading, hero-metric clichés.
- Neon/black "beast mode" gym aesthetics — wrong register entirely.
- Canva-default flatness: the brand's palette and type come from her portfolio, but the
  execution must outclass what Canva can do (craft, motion, layout).
- Washed-out cream minimalism. The tan is a committed mid-tone surface, not a tint.

## Design Principles

1. **Her world, her palette.** Every visual decision derives from the existing
   @mriafitness identity (tan #CFBFA9, deep brown, heavy geometric type, full-stop
   headings). Identity preservation beats novelty.
2. **The photo is the design.** Photo-led sections; imagery is masked/feathered into the
   surface, never boxed. The grey studio shots get a warm wash so the whole page is one
   world.
3. **One ambitious scroll moment.** The "Lean & Locked." letter lock-in is the signature;
   everything else stays restrained (150–300ms hovers, few purposeful reveals).
4. **Never fabricate.** Real facts from her portfolio only. Unknown facts (price,
   inclusions, results) ship as clearly marked placeholders, never invented.
5. **Mobile is the primary canvas.** Instagram traffic lands on a phone; desktop is the
   enhancement.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 (espresso/dark brown on tan — verified 7.35:1), large display
≥3:1. Full keyboard focus visibility, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every
animation (crossfade or static), semantic landmarks, alt text written in brand voice.
